Duck Habits — How to delete your account

Last updated September 24, 2026

In the app (phone or web, at habits-raul.expo.app): Settings > Account and sync > Delete account. The account and all its cloud data are deleted at once.

Without access to the app: send an e-mail to rap.gardini@gmail.com from the account's address asking for deletion. It is done within 15 days and confirmed by e-mail.

What is deleted: e-mail, password, account identifier, all synced data (habits, entries, agenda, goals, name and preferences) and the account's backups. What remains: nothing is used after deletion; only the encrypted backups of the cloud database last up to 90 days, until they are deleted automatically. Data stored on the device stays there until you delete it (Settings > Delete data) or uninstall the app.
